Fretwise

A native iOS guitar companion that listens through the microphone and identifies chords in real time. It combines SwiftUI practice screens, AVFoundation audio capture, tuner tools, lesson progression, and custom DSP for pitch and chord recognition.

What it does

Realtime chord detection

Captures microphone input and classifies chords through pitch, chroma, and chord-template matching.

Guided practice

Lesson steps show the target chord, fretboard shape, tab flow, confidence, and hold-progress feedback.

Tuner mode

Detects the nearest guitar string, displays cents offset, and visualizes tuning accuracy with a gauge.

Build notes

Audio engine

Custom Swift audio services handle capture, pitch detection, chroma extraction, and chord classification.

SwiftUI app

Practice, onboarding, tuner, and lesson views are composed as native SwiftUI screens.

Test coverage

Includes tests for chord libraries, chroma extraction, pitch detection, tuner logic, and lesson flow.
